# Game Guide — AI Text Adventure

An AI-powered text adventure game where every location, story, and interaction is generated by Apple Intelligence running on-device. The world is persistent — locations are generated once and remembered, so the map stays coherent as you explore.

---

## Overview

You play as an adventurer exploring a procedurally-generated world. The AI narrates your journey, generates the world around you, and responds to your actions. Unlike traditional games, you can type or speak any action — the AI understands natural language.

The game runs on Mac/iPad/iPhone using Apple Intelligence (on-device AI). It requires no internet connection.

---

## World Map

### Coordinate System

The world uses a 2D coordinate grid:
- **Origin (0,0)** — your starting location
- **East** = positive X, **West** = negative X
- **South** = positive Y, **North** = negative Y

So moving north from (0,0) takes you to (0,−1); moving east takes you to (1,0).

### Tile Generation

When you enter an unexplored area, the AI generates a **3×3 block of tiles** centred on your new position. Each tile has:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Biome** | The terrain type (forest, plains, desert, mountain, swamp, cave, ocean, ruins, hills, tundra) |
| **Location Name** | A flavourful name for the area |
| **Description** | One evocative sentence describing the place |
| **Atmosphere** | A mood phrase (e.g. "misty, birdsong, cool air") |
| **Landmarks** | 1–2 large immovable features (altar, ruins, statue, well, cave entrance) |
| **Hidden Items** | 5 portable items revealed only by "look around" |

Tiles are generated **once** and stored permanently. Moving south back to a previous location shows the same tile — the world does not re-randomise.

### Biome Transitions

The AI follows coherent transition rules so the world makes geographic sense:

```
forest ↔ plains ↔ hills ↔ mountain
plains ↔ desert
forest ↔ swamp
any ↔ cave (underground)
any ↔ ruins (ancient structures anywhere)
```

Distance from the origin increases difficulty and exoticism.

### Map Tab

The 🗺️ **Map** tab in the sidebar shows a visual grid of explored tiles. Each biome has a distinct colour:

| Biome | Colour |
|---|---|
| Forest | Dark green |
| Plains | Light green |
| Hills | Olive green |
| Mountain | Grey |
| Desert | Gold/yellow |
| Swamp | Dark olive |
| Cave | Brown |
| Ocean | Blue |
| Ruins | Tan |
| Tundra | Steel blue |

Visited tiles are shown at full brightness; unvisited tiles are dimmed. Your current position is marked with a white cross.

## Inventory & Items

### Landmarks vs. Pickups

The game distinguishes two types of entities:

**Landmarks** (inspect only — cannot be picked up):
- Ruins, caves, altars, statues, fountains, wells, towers, bridges, campfires, boulders, etc.
- Shown in the Status tab under **LANDMARKS** in muted italic text
- You can interact with them ("examine the altar", "enter the cave")

**Portable Items** (can be picked up):
- Weapons, armour, food, potions, gems, keys, scrolls
- Shown under **ITEMS** in white text after looking around
- Type "pick up [item name]" to add to your pack

### Item Effects

Every item has an optional effect applied when used or equipped:

| Effect Format | What it does |
|---|---|
| `heal:30` | Restores 30 HP when consumed |
| `food:25` | Reduces hunger by 25 points when eaten |
| `weapon:15` | Equippable — increases combat effectiveness |
| `armor:10` | Equippable — reduces incoming damage by 10 |
| `poison:25` | Touching this entity costs you 25 HP (minus armour) |

### Using Items

- **Eat / Drink / Use / Consume** — for consumables (food, potions)
  - Example: "eat the dried mushroom rations"
- **Equip / Wield / Wear / Put on** — for weapons and armour
  - Example: "equip the carved bone hunting knife"

The AI Game Master interprets your intent and calls the appropriate `use_item` or `equip_item` tool. Consumables are removed from inventory after use.

### Pockets Tab

The 🎒 **Pockets** tab shows your current inventory. Each item displays its effect type (e.g. `⚔️ weapon`, `❤️ heal`, `🍖 food`, `🛡️ armor`, `☠️ poison/danger`).

## Player Stats

Your adventurer has five tracked statistics:

| Stat | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Health** | 0–100 | Reach 0 and you die. Restored by healing items. |
| **Hunger** | 0–100 | 0 = full, 100 = starving. Eat food to reduce. |
| **Energy** | shown as 100−Tiredness | 0 tiredness = fully rested, 100 = exhausted. |
| **Armour** | 0+ | Reduces damage from poison/danger. Increased by equipped armour. |
| **Equipped Weapon** | — | The weapon currently in hand. |
| **Equipped Armour** | — | The armour/protection currently worn. |

### Stat Decay

Each turn (each action you take), your stats decay slightly:
- **Hunger** increases by 2–4 points per turn
- **Tiredness** increases by 1–2 points per turn

### Starvation & Exhaustion Damage

- **Hunger ≥ 80** → lose 2 HP per turn
- **Tiredness ≥ 90** → lose 1 HP per turn

Eat food before you starve. Rest when tired.

### Status Tab

The 📍 **Status** tab shows:
- Current location, biome, and time of day
- Health bar: `❤️ ██████████ 100/100`
- Hunger bar: `🍖 ░░░░░░░░░░ 0/100`
- Energy bar: `😴 ██████████ 100/100`
- Armour value and equipped gear
- Nearby landmarks and items

## Exploration

### Default Behaviour

When you simply move or act, the narrator describes:
- Immediate atmosphere (sounds, smells, light quality)
- Overall mood of the area

Hidden items and detailed surroundings are **not** revealed automatically.

### Look Around

Type **"look around"** (or "examine area", "survey surroundings") to actively explore:
- Reveals all hidden items in the current tile
- Discovered items move from **Hidden** to **ITEMS** list
- Landmarks remain visible throughout (they are always there)

After looking around, items appear in the Status tab and can be picked up.

### Movement

Type directional commands to move:
- "go north" / "go south" / "go east" / "go west"
- "walk north" / "head east" / "move south"
- Or just "north", "south", etc.

The AI Game Master interprets your intent and calls the `move_player` tool, which updates your position and generates new tiles as needed.

## Suggested Actions

Below the narrative text, the game shows **3 suggested action buttons** generated by the AI based on your current context. These suggest contextually appropriate actions:

- "Look around" if you haven't explored yet
- "Go north / south" based on available exits
- "Pick up [item]" if items are visible
- "Examine [landmark]" for interesting features

The AI generates these suggestions at the end of each narrative response. You can also type any free-form action in the input box and press **Send** (or Enter).

## Back to Menu

Tap the **☰** button in the top-left corner of the game screen to return to the main menu. Your game is automatically saved after every turn.
